How a shipment moves

One desk remains accountable while the operating owner and document change by stage.

  1. Rate request receivedWithin two working hours
    One desk Reference number issued
  2. Rate returnedWithin two working days
    The same desk Rate with validity, assumptions and exclusions stated
  3. Booking
    DASA Dubai Booking confirmation, cargo readiness date agreed
  4. Export documentation
    DASA or origin partner Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, DG declaration where applicable
  5. Carriage
    DASA or a named carrier AWB or B/L, tracking shared
  6. Customs clearance
    DASA's own brokerage Customs declaration, duty or exemption applied, release
  7. Delivery
    DASA driver and desk Signed POD, photographs, deviation report if any
  8. Invoice and closeout
    The same desk Invoice matched to quote, file archived
